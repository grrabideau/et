Welcome To My Practice!

I am immensely grateful to belong to a profession I feel passionate about and that allows me to connect with amazing students and their families.

Blending the fields of education and psychology, educational therapy combines educational, therapeutic, and holistic approaches to evaluate and provide intensive individualized intervention and case management for neurodiverse children, adolescents, and young adults who are experiencing learning difficulties.

The primary goal of educational therapy is to provide students an environment in which he/she feels safe, comfortable, and free from anxiety in order to ask questions, take academic risks, learn from mistakes, and thrive to the best of his/her ability. Building rapport and making connections with students and their families is one of the most rewarding aspects of my work.
